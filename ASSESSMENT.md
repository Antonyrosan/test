# English Speaking Assessment

## Baseline

The initial level is a **provisional B1 / B1+ speaking estimate**, based on prior conversation rather than a formal CEFR examination.

### Strengths
- Good technical vocabulary.
- Able to discuss familiar Oracle DBA and workplace topics.
- Can communicate the main idea even when the sentence is not fully polished.

### Priority Areas
- Spontaneous sentence formation.
- Speaking continuously without long pauses.
- Reducing fillers and repeated starts.
- Grammar accuracy while speaking.
- Confidence in professional and casual conversations.
- Thinking in English instead of translating from Tamil.

## Weekly Assessment

At the end of each week, ChatGPT will assess:

1. Fluency
2. Grammar
3. Vocabulary
4. Clarity
5. Pronunciation/intelligibility when voice practice is available
6. Confidence
7. Professional communication
8. Casual conversation ability

## Assessment Format

Each weekly review should include:

- What improved
- What still causes difficulty
- Top 3 recurring mistakes
- Useful corrected sentences
- Score from 1–10 for fluency, grammar, clarity, and confidence
- Comparison with the previous week
- One specific goal for the next week

## First Formal Check

At the end of Week 1, complete a 2-minute spontaneous response about a real work task. Do not prepare a script. The objective is to establish a measurable baseline for fluency, fillers, grammar, and confidence.
