## Day 1 Assessment Summary

- **Grammar baseline:** approximately B1/B1+.
- **Writing:** approximately B1+, with strong technical vocabulary and clear professional structure.
- **Speaking:** approximately B1/B1+, with the ability to explain technical work clearly; fluency is stronger when discussing familiar DBA topics.
- **Listening:** able to understand the main points and ask for repetition/clarification appropriately.
- **Main bottleneck:** spontaneous sentence formation and retrieving natural words/phrases quickly, rather than lack of technical knowledge.
- **Priority grammar:** prepositions, articles, tense consistency, singular/plural agreement, and natural collocations.
- **Priority speaking:** reduce fillers, avoid restarting sentences, keep speaking when a word is missing, and use reusable sentence patterns.
- **Priority writing:** workplace emails, incident summaries, status updates, documentation, and colleague chat messages.

## Scoring Guide

- **Fluency:** How continuously and naturally you speak.
- **Grammar:** Accuracy of sentence structure and tense.
- **Confidence:** How comfortable and decisive the speaking feels.
- **Meeting Score:** Ability to communicate clearly in a simulated professional situation.
- **Fillers:** Track words such as “uh”, “um”, “yeah”, and repeated sentence starts.
