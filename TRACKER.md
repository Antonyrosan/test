## Scoring Guide

- **Fluency:** How continuously and naturally you speak.
- **Grammar:** Accuracy of sentence structure and tense.
- **Confidence:** How comfortable and decisive the speaking feels.
- **Meeting Score:** Ability to communicate clearly in a simulated professional situation.
- **Fillers:** Track words such as “uh”, “um”, “yeah”, and repeated sentence starts.
